MinTube 1.0.10 adds an Updates screen.

## New

- **Updates screen.** Open it from Settings → Updates, or from About → Updates. Settings shows whether MinTube is up to date.
  - **App update** shows whether you are current, the installed version and the time of the last successful check, with a Check for updates button. When a newer version exists it shows what changed and the download size, and Download and install handles the rest as before.
  - **Automatically check for updates** checks about once a day while you are online.
  - **Update notifications** posts a notification when a newer version is found. Tapping it opens the App update page. Android 13 and later asks for notification permission when you turn this on or tap Download and install; MinTube keeps working if you decline.
  - **Share app APK** sends the installed MinTube package to another app or device.
  - **Changelogs** lists recent releases with their date and age. Tap one to read its notes; if it is newer than your version, you can install it from there.
- The update check moved out of About. About now has an Updates row that opens the new screen.
- Vietnamese and English text for the new screens.
