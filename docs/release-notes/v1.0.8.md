MinTube 1.0.8 filters search results before showing them and fixes a wrong publish time.

## Fixed

- **Filtered searches no longer jump.** With an Upload date selected, results used to appear and then disappear as their dates arrived. MinTube now gets every missing publish time first, removes videos older than the limit, and only then shows the list. A page with nothing left loads the next one (up to three).
- **Search results and the video page agree on publish time.** A video published 9 hours ago could read "1 day ago" in search when signed in, because the time of day was dropped. The full time is now kept.
