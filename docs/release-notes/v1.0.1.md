MinTube 1.0.1 fixes a playback regression in 1.0.0.

## Fixed

- **Region-restricted videos failed to play over a VPN.** In 1.0.0, "Trending in Vietnam" (on by default) sent the Vietnam region with every request, including the request that fetches the video stream. When content is blocked in Vietnam and you watch it through a VPN in another country, YouTube returned stream URLs that the video server then rejected (HTTP 403, "Could not play this video").
  - The region setting now applies only to trending, Home, Explore and search, which is what the setting is for.
  - Playback requests use the default region again, as Litube does.
  - Workaround on 1.0.0: turn off Settings > General > Trending in Vietnam.

## Other

- More reliable device tests for the download options sheet.
