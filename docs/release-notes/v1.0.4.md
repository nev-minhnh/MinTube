MinTube 1.0.4 separates the status bar from the content on tablets.

## Changed

- **A strip under the status bar on wide screens.** When the navigation sits on the left (a tablet in landscape), a translucent strip in the same colour as the navigation now lies under the status bar, with a thin line below it. The clock and notification icons no longer sit on top of the content. The strip is hidden in fullscreen video.
