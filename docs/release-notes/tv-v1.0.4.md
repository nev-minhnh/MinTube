MinTube TV 1.0.4 filters search results before showing them and fixes a wrong publish time.

## Fixed

- **Filtered searches no longer jump.** With an Upload date selected, MinTube TV gets every missing publish time first, removes videos older than the limit, and only then shows the results.
- **Search results and the video page agree on publish time.** The time of day is kept, so a video published 9 hours ago no longer reads "1 day ago".
