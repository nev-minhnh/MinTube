MinTube 1.0.0 is the first release. It is a fork of [Litube](https://github.com/HydeYYHH/litube) 4.0.0 by HydeYYHH, licensed under GPL-3.0. MinTube keeps Litube's playback, extraction, download and account code and replaces the interface and the app identity.

MinTube installs alongside Litube: its application ID is `com.nevminhnh.mintube`, so it never replaces Litube. Settings backups from Litube can be restored in MinTube. Downloads are still saved under `Downloads/Litube`.

## What's new

- **Redesigned interface** on every screen, in light and dark themes:
  - cyan accent, true-grey neutrals, Be Vietnam Pro titles and Material Symbols icons;
  - one card family across all feeds;
  - loading skeletons, plus empty and error states with a retry button.
- **New icon:** a gradient "m" with a play mark, including a themed (monochrome) layer.
- **Watch page:**
  - The player stays pinned at the top while you scroll.
  - When the title scrolls away, a "now playing" strip shows the title and channel.
  - A related-video filter (All / From the channel) sits below the strip.
  - A soft glow in the video's colours spreads behind the player. You can turn it off in Settings > Playback.
  - The seek bar is inset from the screen edges and its played part shows a cyan-to-pink gradient.
- **Phones and tablets:**
  - Phones stay in portrait; only fullscreen video rotates.
  - Tablets rotate freely.
  - In landscape, tablets get a navigation rail and a two-column watch page whose columns scroll independently.
- **Content language and region** (Settings > General):
  - "Translate titles to English" is on by default, which keeps YouTube's English auto-translated titles. Turn it off to get titles in the app's language, and the watch page then shows the creator's original title and description.
  - "Trending in Vietnam" is on by default, so trending, Home, Explore and search use the Vietnam region. Turn it off to use the device's region.
- **Gestures:**
  - Flick down on the video to minimize it into the mini-player, on phones and tablets.
  - The brightness and volume swipes work only in fullscreen, never on the embedded player or the mini-player.
- **Mini-player:**
  - Inside the app it is fixed at the bottom-right and can't be dragged.
  - Leaving the app from any screen while a video plays switches to picture-in-picture.
- **Fixes on top of Litube 4.0.0:**
  - Remote search suggestions work again.
  - Channel avatars now show on the Home feed for signed-out users.
  - The download options sheet opens taller.
  - History shows when it is paused.
  - Player controls are labelled for screen readers.

## Verification

All tests run locally. The results for this release are committed under `test-results/`. They cover unit tests, lint, debug/release/R8 builds and the offline emulator suite. Two device tests, `PipDismissAndroidTest` (expanding the system picture-in-picture menu) and `FullscreenSheetAndroidTest` (sheets in fullscreen), were set aside by the owner for manual testing and are excluded from the release gate. QA results and screenshots are in `docs/QA_REPORT.md` and `docs/screens/`.

## Known limitations

- Features that YouTube's data doesn't provide are not offered. These include a sleep timer, seek-bar thumbnails, a notification bell, and playlist rename, delete or reorder. See the missing list in `docs/FEATURE_MATRIX.md`.
- With "Translate titles to English" turned off, lists can still show YouTube's translation into the app language (only the watch page shows the original title), and TV-style rows lose the "views · time ago" line. The second limit comes from MediaServiceCore. See `docs/CONTENT_LOCALE.md`.
- Account features were not verified on a signed-in account during QA.
- The second QA pass ran at phone size only. Tablet layouts were checked during development and in the release smoke test, not in a full QA pass. 43 of the 140 checked feature rows are marked "not verifiable" in `docs/FEATURE_MATRIX.md`: about 20 of them need sign-in, and the rest could not be exercised because the test machine was overloaded.
