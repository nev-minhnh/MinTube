MinTube 1.0.6 fixes the Upload date filter and changes how publish time reads.

## Fixed

- **The Upload date filter no longer fails when you are signed in.** Choosing Today, This week, This month or This year showed "Something went wrong" for signed-in accounts, because YouTube sends those results without its filter options. MinTube now sends the filter itself.
  - Videos whose date only arrives after the results load are filtered again once the date is known, so nothing older than the limit stays in the list.
  - Ages YouTube rounds ("3 weeks ago", "2 years ago") are also checked against the limit.

## Changed

- **Publish time uses whole units.** When YouTube gives the real date:
  - Under 12 months, the age uses the largest unit that fits exactly: 24 hours shows as 1 day but 23 or 25 hours stay in hours; 7 days is 1 week but 6 or 8 days stay in days; 30 days is 1 month.
  - Older than 12 months, the date shows as dd/mm/yyyy.
  - A video dated today with no time shows "Today".
  - When YouTube only sends rounded text ("2 years ago"), that text is kept, since the exact date is unknown.
