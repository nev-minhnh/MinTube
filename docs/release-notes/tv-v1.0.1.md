MinTube TV 1.0.1 fixes three things reported on real TVs.

## Changes

- **Back in the player.** With the seek bar or the controls on screen (after OK, a pause or a seek), or the settings or "Up next" row open, Back now only hides them: the video stays full screen and keeps playing, or stays paused. Back leaves the video only when nothing is shown over it.
- **Smaller menu and search bar.** The menu on the left is a narrow column of icons when closed and shows its labels only while you are in it, so Home, Subscriptions, Library and Settings get more room. The search bar is a single slim line with small round buttons for clear and voice search.
- **Home stays as you left it.** Home no longer reloads by itself when you come back from a video, switch between menu items or return to the app; the rows and the selected video stay. To get new videos, choose **Refresh** at the bottom of the menu: Home reloads and the first new video is selected.

## Install

Download `MinTube-TV-v1.0.1.apk` from this release and sideload it over 1.0.0 (same app, same signing key); see the "MinTube TV" section of the README.
