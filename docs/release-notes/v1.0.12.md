MinTube 1.0.12 shows view counts in one short form everywhere.

## Changed

- **Short view counts on every video.** Cards, lists, Shorts, playlists, channel pages, search, history and the watch page now all read view counts the same short way, in the app language: "188K views" in English, "188 N lượt xem" in Vietnamese. Before, cards showed the full number ("188,491 views") while the watch page showed the short one.
  - Counts under 10 thousand, million or billion keep one decimal ("1.2M views", "1,2 Tr lượt xem").
  - Live "watching" counts and other labels are left as YouTube sends them.
