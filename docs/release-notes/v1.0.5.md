MinTube 1.0.5 shows how long ago each video was published.

## New

- **Publish time on every video.** Video cards and rows now end with when the video went up, for example "Channel · 1.2M views · 5 days ago". The text follows the app language ("5 ngày trước" in Vietnamese) and is worked out for each video from what YouTube returns. A video that only carries a date shows the time since that date.
  - Home (including the tablet grid), Explore, Subscriptions, Search, related videos, the video details, channel Videos and Live tabs, search inside a channel, playlists, and History when you are signed in.
  - History kept on the device, local playlists and Watch later have no publish time to show.
- **The Upload date filter in Search now checks the publish time.** With Today, This week, This month or This year selected, MinTube also removes videos older than the limit, so results match the filter. Channels, playlists and videos without a publish time are kept.
