MinTube TV 1.0.8 shows more on video cards and centres the Settings pages.

## Changed

- **Video cards.** Cards now carry the title, the channel name, the view count and the publish time wherever the data has them. History entries from your account keep their publish time, and the Shorts and Live tabs on a channel page show the channel name.
- **Cards update in place.** When the app fills in a missing view count or publish time after a page is shown, the card updates without reloading the list.
- **Centred Settings.** At Interface sizes of 75% and smaller, Settings and Updates were pushed to the left. They are now a centred column with equal margins on both sides at every Interface size. At 100% the layout is unchanged apart from the balanced margins.
