MinTube TV 1.0.2 shows how long ago each video was published.

## New

- **Publish time on every video card.** Cards read "Channel · 1.2M views · 5 days ago", in the app language ("5 ngày trước" in Vietnamese), worked out for each video from what YouTube returns.
  - Home, Search, Subscriptions, channel Videos and Live, Watch later, Liked, playlist details, Up next and the autoplay card. The Info panel shows the date in the app language.
  - History and channel Shorts have no publish time to show.
- **The Upload date filter in Search now checks the publish time.** With Today, This week, This month or This year selected, videos older than the limit are removed, so results match the filter.

## Also from the phone app

- Home keeps its videos until you press Refresh on the left rail (from 1.0.1), and the fixes shipped in MinTube 1.0.2 to 1.0.5.
