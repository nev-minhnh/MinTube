MinTube 1.0.2 keeps Home the way you left it.

## Fixed

- **Home no longer refreshes on its own.** Coming back to Home (pressing Back from a video, switching tabs, or returning to the app) used to replace the video list after you watched something or after 15 minutes. Home now keeps the same videos and the same scroll position until you ask for new ones.
  - Home still reloads when it has to: switching accounts, signing in or out, or changing the content language or region.

## Changed

- **Pull to refresh for new recommendations.** Drag down from the top of Home until the refresh icon appears, then release. Releasing early cancels. After a successful refresh, Home scrolls back to the top. Subscriptions and Explore work the same way.
