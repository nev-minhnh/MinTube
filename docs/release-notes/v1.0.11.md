MinTube 1.0.11 fills in view counts and publish times on video cards that arrive without them.

## Fixed

- **Home, Subscriptions and Explore cards now show views and publish time.** Signed-in feeds often omit them, so cards showed only the channel name. MinTube now reads the missing details in the background, the same way search results do, and fills them in place. The page still appears immediately; only rows missing views or a publish time are read, and live streams and Shorts are skipped.
- The same repair applies to the related list on the watch page, the channel Videos tab and playlist rows.
- **Card layout.** The channel name is on its own line, with one "views · time" line under it. A missing part no longer leaves a stray separator.
- **Explore: empty "Quick pick radio" cards.** Radio and mix entries that arrive without a title are no longer drawn as blank boxes. A mix without a cover borrows the thumbnail of its first video.

## Notes

- The details come from the same anonymous player read search uses, are cached for the session (500 videos) and run at most four at a time. A video whose read fails keeps what it had and is not retried until you switch account.
- Like counts are not shown on cards.
